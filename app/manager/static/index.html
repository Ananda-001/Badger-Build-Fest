Model routing

Every request goes to the primary model first. If it is rate-limited or returns unusable output, the router switches to the next backup immediately. Backup answers are delivered but held for review; if every model is unavailable, the request is escalated to a person. Figures are from a 300-request load test on Databricks Free Edition, where rate limits are low.

Suggestions the maintainer record cannot settle, so a person decides. Each decision is saved to Databricks and counts toward automating that kind of case: once a kind is proven, matching suggestions resolve on their own.

How to read a suggestion
  1. Type: what the model proposes: a duplicate, part of a larger effort, or a related ticket.
  2. Suggested by: the model, and its self-reported confidence. Confidence is the model's own estimate, not evidence.
  3. Tickets: the new ticket and the earlier ticket it points to. IDs open in Jira.
  4. Model's reason: in the model's own words.
  5. Similar past cases: how reviewers and maintainers decided this kind of case before, and how many more consistent decisions would let Assay resolve it automatically.
  6. Your decision: Accept applies the link, Reject keeps the tickets separate, Skip moves it to the end. Saved to the Delta table actions; undo within six seconds.

Why confident answers fail

Answers a model rated at least 95% confident that the maintainer record contradicts, grouped by what went wrong. This is why Assay never lets a model's confidence decide on its own.
